# 0002 — Round-Robin Starting Numbers, Round-Count Adjustment, and Draw Reset

**Status: Approved** (by the owner, 2026-10-16)  
**Date:** 2026-10-16  
**Depends on:** Accepted spec 0001 and the owner's follow-up decisions on 2026-10-16.  
**Amended:** 2026-10-16 — four owner-approved clarifications: one-stage Settings recovery after Clear, retained inactive-player drafts, editable batch table, and ordered confirmations.  
**UI languages:** Ukrainian (default), English. No Russian UI.

## 1. Purpose and scope

Improve the referee's round-robin workflow without adding another pairing algorithm.

1. A round-robin participant gets a **starting number** in their player card **and** in an editable, batch-save starting-number table on Pairings; both refer to the same tournament data.
2. The referee can either draw using the entered starting numbers or **randomly reassign all** starting numbers, through two explicit choices. Implicit registration-order seeding is removed.
3. When a terminal/full round-robin stage has an incorrect total round count, starting generation offers a **separate confirmation** to adjust the number of rounds to the exact Berger cycle.
4. **Clear round-robin draw** removes all RR-stage pairings, including byes and manually entered pairings, if the RR stage has **no entered game results**. It releases the frozen roster and starting-number assignment for further editing. If clearing also leaves **one stage and no pairings anywhere**, it restores the ability to change the initial system in Settings (an explicit exception to 0001).
5. The round-robin **Generate** action becomes unavailable as soon as any game in that RR stage has an entered result, and becomes available again if the referee explicitly clears every such result by manual editing. A generated bye (+1) is **not** an entered game result for this lock.

The existing Berger pairing formula, fixed-color assignment, bye scoring (+1 win/point), scoring across stage boundaries, XML export limits, and rating-within-score-groups view remain as in accepted 0001.

**Out of scope:** Automatic or altered Swiss, McMahon and Scheveningen pairing; clear/generate workflows for those systems; manual protection of RR pairings; automatic handicap, automatic technical results, removing stage boundaries, and adding RR stages after non-RR stages.

## 2. Manual starting numbers in the player card and Pairings table

### 2.1. UI and stored data

When the tournament contains a round-robin stage, the Player Editor includes:

- **UA:** «Стартовий номер»
- **EN:** “Starting number”

This is an optional positive integer while preparing a draw. It belongs to a **tournament-local participant in that RR stage**, not to the global catalog, player rating or registration ID. The 0002 supported scope has at most one RR stage (a later RR stage after another system remains deferred).

- The referee may save partially filled starting numbers while preparing the tournament, including temporary duplicates while swapping assigned numbers.
- While the stage is unfrozen, eligible participants' draft numbers can be edited or cleared **either in their player card or in a compact table on Pairings**. Both surfaces read and write the same persisted draft mapping.
- The Pairings table shows **player → starting number**, supports multiple edits and **one atomic batch save** (with no intermediate per-row saves that could incorrectly flag a temporary duplicate), and visibly distinguishes eligible from ineligible players. Draft rows may be incomplete or temporarily duplicated; final sequence validation happens at generation (§2.2).
- After a successful RR generation, the actual assignment is frozen; **both** the player-card field and Pairings table display it **read-only** until an explicit successful Clear RR Draw (§5).
- An RR player must be finalized and eligible at the RR stage start to be in the numbered roster. A preliminary or later-joining participant's field may show their **previously saved draft number**, but it is **disabled with an explanation** in both places; their draft is **retained**, ignored for RR generation and does **not** count toward N, produce duplicate/gap errors or reserve a starting number. Do **not** auto-clear it when the player becomes ineligible.
- If such a player becomes eligible **before the first/following unfrozen draw**, their retained draft becomes editable and participates in the ordinary `1..N` validation again, possibly requiring correction. No automatic renumbering.
- Changes to names, rank, rating or eligibility must never silently change frozen starting numbers.

Use a **single consistent authority**: before draw, persist editable draft numbers on the appropriate tournament-local participant/stage association; both editors update this same draft data, with the batch endpoint validating and saving all requested edits together. Once drawn, the ordered `PairingStage.FrozenRosterIds` is the canonical immutable Berger ordering. Its 1-based indices define starting numbers displayed in **both** the player card and Pairings table. Ensure projections/persisted values cannot disagree; reconcile older data saved by 0001 using the frozen list rather than inventing a new ordering. After clearing, retain the visible assignment as editable draft numbers. Do not duplicate the starting numbers into the shared player catalog.

### 2.2. Validation when the referee chooses entered numbers

For an RR stage with N **eligible finalized** members, a draw using entered numbers requires **exactly** the integers `1, 2, …, N` occurring once each, with no omissions, gaps or duplicates **among those N members only**. Drafts held by preliminary or later-stage-only players are excluded from this validation, even if duplicated or outside the current `1..N` range.

Reject the draw atomically (no frozen roster, new pairings or changed numbers) if:

- A participant has no assigned number: identify the **player** and their empty field.
- Numbers are repeated: identify the **number and every affected player**.
- A number is outside `1..N`: identify the **player, value and valid range**.
- The set has a gap: identify the **missing number(s)** and, as applicable, players with incorrect/out-of-range assignments.

The server must return structured, localized errors with the relevant player IDs/names and erroneous numbers; the UI presents actionable feedback next to the relevant player field(s) in the card/**batch table** and/or an accessible linked error summary. Do **not** silently compact numbers, infer missing values from registration order or generate pairings from a partial assignment.

Validation applies at generation time. Saving an unfinished player card should not be blocked merely because other cards have no number yet; invalid/noninteger field input itself should receive normal field validation.

### 2.3. Old 0001 tournaments

For an RR stage already generated by 0001, derive the visible per-player numbers from its `FrozenRosterIds` and keep them locked. Old stage data must load without changing any existing pairings, colors, IDs or results. An undrawn RR stage from 0001 has no assigned draft numbers by default; **do not** silently treat registration IDs as starting numbers when generating under 0002. The referee must assign them manually or opt for random allocation.

Persist the new draft data with backward-compatible defaults; do not change shared catalog schema unless technically necessary.

## 3. Random allocation versus entered numbers

The existing checkbox remains available **before** RR generation while the assignment is not frozen:

- **UA:** «Присвоїти стартові номери випадково»
- **EN:** “Assign starting numbers randomly”

The owner's requested choice is explicit:

- **“Use entered numbers”** — leave all entered values unchanged; validate a complete unique sequence as in §2.2, then draw the Berger schedule using ascending starting numbers.
- **“Draw randomly and overwrite numbers”** — ignore and replace **all** existing draft starting-number values for eligible RR participants with one Fisher–Yates (or equivalent unbiased) permutation of `1..N`, then draw from that permutation.

When any eligible participant already has a manually entered number and random assignment is selected, present a **two-action choice dialog** with these two alternatives **as the first confirmation**, before any round-count-adjustment dialog or committed changes. Make it explicit that random assignment overwrites **all** entered numbers, not only missing ones. The referee may cancel the dialog with no changes. If no numbers have been entered, selecting the random checkbox may proceed directly; an explicit action must still make the chosen mode clear.

If “Use entered numbers” is chosen but entries are partial/invalid, report the exact validation errors; do not fall back to random automatically. If “Draw randomly and overwrite” is chosen, previously incomplete or duplicated drafts are no barrier to randomization.

Random numbers, their actual mapping, any confirmed round-count adjustment and the generated fixtures must commit atomically. A canceled dialog, failed generation, count-adjustment refusal or any later validation conflict must **not** partially overwrite entered numbers. Generating another round of an already frozen stage uses that frozen assignment and does not offer a redraw. The Standings “sort by rating within score groups” checkbox never influences the starting numbers.

After a successful clear (§5), the numbers last used — including formerly random ones — remain visible as **editable drafts**. Choosing random again generates a **new** assignment; choosing entered uses the visible existing assignment after validation.

## 4. Prompt to adjust total number of RR rounds

### 4.1. Applicability and calculation

When the referee invokes RR generation for a **terminal RR stage** (no following pairing stage), calculate the exact one-cycle number of rounds from its eligible finalized roster:

- Even N: `N − 1`.
- Odd N: `N`.

If the configured total round count differs, do **not** return only the old generic mismatch error. Offer a **separate explicit confirmation dialog** showing the participant count, configured rounds and required rounds:

- **UA (illustrative):** «У турнірі 8 учасників. Для кругової системи потрібно 7 турів, зараз налаштовано 5. Змінити кількість турів на 7 і виконати жеребкування?»
- **EN:** equivalent localized copy.

**Confirm:** validate the resize and draw as **one atomic server transaction**. **Cancel:** change nothing: no change of round count, existing participant data, draft starting numbers, frozen roster or pairings. The same prompt applies if the configured count is **too large** or **too small**.

Do not silently change the number of rounds while editing tournament Settings or player cards.

For an RR stage intentionally ending at a **later non-RR stage boundary**, do not resize the overall tournament or move the stage boundary. Preserve the agreed 0001 prefix-of-Berger behavior. Reject an RR stage longer than one Berger cycle with an actionable error; do not add a second cycle.

### 4.2. Safety of round-count changes

Prevalidate before any write:

- Do not remove an existing result, a credited bye, a paired game, retained historical round data or legacy metadata merely to reduce the round count.
- Do not invalidate a stage boundary, a participant's first eligible round, or availability settings by shortening.
- Do not drop or silently rewrite future pairings, even if unplayed; if the count adjustment would do so, report the affected rounds and stop without mutations. The referee may resolve those entries manually and retry.
- Respect `TournamentEditor.MaxRounds` and existing global settings validation.
- On extension, create no phantom participants or scoring outcomes; available newly numbered rounds are initially empty apart from the requested atomic generation.

If a safe adjustment is impossible, explain why and leave **both** the existing round count and draw intact. **Dialog order is fixed:** first resolve the starting-number choice (use entered vs randomly overwrite, when required by §3); **second** ask for the round-count adjustment (when required by §4). Only **after both** have been approved does the UI submit **one** final atomic request carrying the selected number source and the authorized target round count. Canceling **either** dialog leaves all data unchanged; no intermediate update of draft numbers, round count, stage or pairings is permitted. The backend rechecks all invariants on that final request and rejects any stale/unsafe state without partial writes.

Keep the selected/current round valid after a successful resize; do not silently switch pairing systems or modify non-RR stage history.

## 5. Clear round-robin draw

### 5.1. Action, scope and confirmation

On the Pairings screen for an RR stage add:

- **UA:** «Очистити жеребкування»
- **EN:** “Clear round-robin draw”

This is an explicit **stage-level action**, not “delete selected round” and not a direct edit to `Tournament.System`.

Before clearing, show a confirmation that lists the affected RR rounds, how many pairings (including byes) will be removed, that bye-awarded wins/points will disappear, and that **manual unplayed pairings and their metadata will also be deleted**. There is **no “Protect all” or manual `Protected` exception** for RR; the owner confirmed that *everything* is deleted if there are no entered game results. A cancellation is a no-op.

On confirmation, atomically:

1. Remove **all pairings** in all rounds of **that RR stage only**, including normal games, byes and manually created/unplayed games, regardless of any `Protected` flag. Do not affect any later Swiss/McMahon/Scheveningen stage.
2. Clear `FrozenRosterIds` and the frozen/random-assignment status for the RR stage, returning it to the pre-draw state.
3. Preserve the current participant→starting-number mapping as **editable drafts**, including numbers previously assigned randomly; future generation can reuse them or explicitly overwrite them with new random numbers.
4. Recompute scores, bye points, standings and preparation views from the remaining authoritative results. Removal of RR byes subtracts their previously credited wins/points automatically. Never add a separate manual adjustment to the totals.
5. Make the RR roster editable again subject to any **other** saved stage and result constraints. No other stage, participant catalog entry, later-stage result or completed game changes.
6. **Explicit 0002 exception to the 0001 Settings lock:** only as part of this **successful Clear RR Draw** operation, if the resulting tournament has **exactly one stage and no pairings anywhere in any round**, reset `PairingsEverDrawn=false`. Thus the Settings system selector is unlocked and the referee can correct an accidentally chosen RR system for round 1 (e.g. select Swiss). After a change, the single stage and `Tournament.System` must be updated consistently by the normal Settings operation. If **either** condition is false (multiple stages or any remaining pairing), retain `PairingsEverDrawn=true` and keep Settings locked.

**This unlock is specific to explicit clearing, not to arbitrary deletion.** The 0001 historical lock remains in force for manually deleting individual pairings, importing or migrating old tournaments, and all other actions; simply deleting all pairings one by one must **not** unlock Settings. Even if the referee has manually cleared all results before the Clear action, only the successful Clear operation can apply the exception. A canceled or refused Clear never changes `PairingsEverDrawn`. When the tournament has multiple stages, clearing the RR prefix must **not** unlock the global system selector or modify any later-stage boundary. This is the sole intentional non-monotonic exception to the 0001 historical flag.

### 5.2. What counts as an entered result

For RR re-generation/clear locking, use the existence of a saved game result **on a two-player pairing** in **any round of the RR stage**, including `BlackWins`, `WhiteWins`, `Draw`, `BothLose`, or an imported non-`Unset`/unknown result. A technical/referee-decided game with a recorded result also counts.

**A regular generated bye is not an entered game result**, even though it immediately earns one win and one point under 0001. Do not use `HasAwardedOutcome` unconditionally as the RR draw lock: that would falsely freeze every odd-roster tournament at generation. Preserve `HasAwardedOutcome` for existing per-pairing score protection and stage-change rules outside this narrowly scoped RR reset.

- If at least one entered RR game result exists, disable/reject **Clear RR Draw** and **Generate RR**, with a clear localized reason. No special “force” or “protect games” option is available.
- If the referee **manually removes every entered game result**, both actions become available again. This lock is derived from **current** results, not a permanent `everHadResult` latch. The referee has the right to correct a mistaken result.
- Existing explicit manual editing/deletion of individual games and results keeps its normal safety prompts. The new stage-level clear must **never** overwrite or discard a recorded game result.
- A bye, including its credited point, does **not** by itself prevent clear. The stage-level confirmation explicitly covers deletion of all those awarded bye points.

Check the lock on the backend inside the serialized tournament mutation even if the UI button is disabled. The UI must show the reason instead of allowing an action that would fail without explanation.

### 5.3. Implication of the strict RR generation lock

The owner chose to lock **round-robin generation itself** after the first entered game result. This also means the referee cannot generate *as-yet undrawn later RR rounds* until all results have been manually cleared. **Do not silently relax this rule.**

To prevent surprise, when generating only one RR round before the entire stage is scheduled, show an informational warning that the referee should generate all required RR rounds **before entering the first result**, or otherwise be ready to finish by manual editing. The existing unchecked “Generate all round-robin rounds” control remains the convenient way to generate the entire schedule before play begins.

Other pairing systems are unaffected: their future generation/reset behavior will be handled in later specs, and any implemented manual editing is unchanged. This spec must not introduce or enable a Swiss/McMahon/Scheveningen automatic generator.

## 6. API, state transitions and compatibility

Introduce narrowly scoped domain operations/endpoints for:

- Editing/saving draft RR starting numbers in the participant card **and via a bulk, atomic starting-number table save on Pairings**; server eligibility checks must be identical for both.
- RR draw generation with an explicit **source choice** (entered numbers vs overwrite-randomly), replacing the old implicit registration-order fallback. Preserve the existing selected-round / all-round choice.
- An **atomic confirm-round-count-and-generate** path (not “save settings, then maybe generate”).
- A **preflight/clear RR stage** path with atomic validation and explicit confirmation, including the **conditional** one-stage/no-pairings Settings unlock.

The UI may use server-returned confirmation-needed responses or a server preview plus commit request, but the dialog order is **numbers first, round count second**, and each final write must **revalidate** the same conditions and be transactional. Never require the browser to compute Berger scheduling or scoring rules; keep those in the domain. Reject stale/unsafe requests rather than partially applying them.

Persist draft numbers and frozen assignments in the tournament's JSON with safe defaults for older 0000/0001 saves. On 0001 RR tournaments with frozen rosters, reflect `FrozenRosterIds` into **both player-card and Pairings-table views**; on clear preserve the last effective mapping and thaw it. Maintain the 0001 cross-stage and late-entrant restrictions and Settings system lock **except for the explicitly approved conditional reset on successful Clear in §5.1**.

Legacy XML has no verified RR `Kind` and no starting-number schema mapping: do not invent those. For exportable mixed tournaments, explicitly include any new manual-starting-number or draft-number limitations in the existing round-trip export report. Preserve actual fixtures and results.

The new UI text, field validation, dialogs and errors must be localized in **uk/en** with key parity. Keep the approved 0000 and 0001 spec documents immutable; document explicit 0002 overrides in `docs/decisions.md`, update architecture/status when implementing.

## 7. Acceptance and regression tests

### 7.1. Starting numbers

1. An RR player card exposes the optional starting number and Pairings provides an **editable, atomic batch-save number table** before draw; both show the same values, both are read-only after draw, and neither is required in the global catalog or non-RR-only tournaments.
2. Save partial/manual draft numbers while registering players **or in a single bulk table submission**, including temporary duplicate drafts. Attempt generation with an empty number, duplicate, gap, out-of-range number **among eligible players**: no writes, actionable error identifying the player(s) and number(s). Correcting the numbers allows generation.
3. A valid manually assigned permutation defines the frozen Berger order **regardless of registration order or ratings**; the same mapping survives reload and an out-of-order per-round generation.
4. Random-overwrite replaces *all* eligible players' entered numbers only after the two-choice confirmation; canceling preserves all drafts; “use entered” validates and preserves them. One random assignment is frozen and later rounds reuse it. **Inactive players' retained draft numbers remain untouched.**
5. Previously generated 0001 RR tournaments show correct read-only numbers derived from their frozen roster; no fixtures or results are modified by migration. Undrawn 0001 RR tournaments do not silently auto-number players.

### 7.2. Round-count adjustment

6. Eight players and five configured rounds: a separate prompt offers seven rounds. Confirm performs resize + generation atomically; cancel preserves five rounds and all draft state.
7. Eight players and ten configured rounds: prompt offers seven; if any trimmed round contains a pairing, result, relevant stage boundary, participant joining round or important retained metadata, the attempt refuses safely and changes nothing. Safe shrink works.
8. Odd N requires N rounds; RR with later Swiss stage leaves total rounds and boundaries unchanged and uses only the RR prefix.
9. Invalid starting numbers, a canceled overwrite dialog, a failed count adjustment or a late validation conflict never writes half a random permutation, stage roster, count change or pairings. Where both prompts are needed, the **number-source choice appears first**, then **round-count confirmation**, and **only one server transaction** occurs after both approvals.

### 7.3. Clear and lock lifecycle

10. No entered RR results: confirm Clear RR Draw removes all pairs in the stage, **including manual unplayed pairs, `Protected` imports and all byes**, without touching later-stage rounds. Cancel changes nothing.
11. A five-player RR with generated byes can be cleared immediately; credited bye scores disappear from standings and pre-round groups. The fact that a bye earned one point does **not** block clearing.
12. Clear releases the frozen roster and allows editing starting numbers and eligibility again. The last manual/random numbers remain as editable drafts; a new random draw can overwrite them.
13. Enter any two-player result in any RR round, including a referee-decision result or imported unknown result: both RR Generate and Clear become unavailable. Backend API rejects both even if called directly. No partial deletion is possible.
14. Remove **every** entered game result manually: generation and clear become available again. A retained generated bye alone does not lock them.
15. One RR round generated with remaining rounds empty: once the first game result is entered, generation of *even those empty later RR rounds* is blocked, as specified; UI warns of this consequence before entering the first result.
16. A successful RR Clear with **one stage and no remaining pairings anywhere** resets `PairingsEverDrawn` and unlocks Settings so that RR can be changed back to Swiss for round 1. Settings changes update the sole stage consistently; the roster and numbers are editable again. Clear in a **multi-stage tournament** or where **any pairing remains** retains the flag and Settings lock, including after reload. **Deleting all pairings individually without invoking Clear** does not reset the flag or unlock Settings.
17. Any existing game with a recorded result is never deleted/changed by a generation or clear operation, even if the client lies about confirmations. The referee's manual editor remains the only way to remove/correct results.

### 7.4. Regression and delivery

18. Full Berger fixture identity, colour balance, odd-roster byes (+1), technical results, stage-change boundary, opponent history, saved standings and rating-within-score-groups order from 0001 remain unchanged where the new lifecycle does not apply.
19. Existing 0000/0001 SQLite tournament saves and legacy imported tournaments load without data loss; non-RR-only tournaments see no new RR controls or changed generation logic.
20. Verify uk/en field/dialog wording, **batch-table editing and disabling**, error focus or linked summary, **numbers-first then round-count** preview and cancel flows, and round-count rollback in browser-level tests.
21. An inactive (preliminary or later-joining) participant retains a visible disabled draft field with an explanation, does not consume a number, and cannot produce duplicates or gaps during a draw. Upon becoming eligible before the draw, their retained number re-enters validation; it is **not** silently cleared or renumbered.
22. Bulk-save multiple manual numbers from Pairings atomically; show the same values in individual player cards after reload. A batch with invalid input (e.g. noninteger) must not partially update other rows, and ordinary partial/duplicate drafts are allowed before generation.
23. Explicitly clearing an eligible **single-stage** RR draw and then changing Settings to Swiss in round 1 works and survives reload. A regular last-pair deletion without Clear does **not** unlock Settings; neither does Clear when a later stage exists or a pairing remains anywhere. Canceling Clear never unlocks it.
24. When both dialogs are needed, verify their order, that canceling **either** preserves participant numbers and round count, and that the accepted operation produces a single atomic persisted roster and adjusted schedule.

Run the backend/frontend Docker test profiles, frontend typecheck/build, and a short bilingual browser smoke test with **disposable tournament data only**. Do not deploy without the owner's separate instruction.

## 8. Approval gate

This is a new **Proposed** spec 0002, not an amendment to accepted spec 0001. It intentionally supersedes 0001's **implicit registration-order starting numbers**, its **permissive re-generation around existing played RR games**, and **only on an explicit successful single-stage, pairings-free Clear**, the otherwise monotonic Settings lock. It retains all other 0001 invariants.

Do not implement until the owner approves this spec. If a technical limitation requires weakening a referee-facing rule above, ask the owner rather than silently substituting a different behavior.
